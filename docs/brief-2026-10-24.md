## Booking (Victor, 24 October: "a free booking part of the app for Frank, for everyone")

- **For the visitor:** Frank tab > Book a session (or the link `#book.session`, for his
  Instagram bio). No account, no membership, no payment. Pick a time, first name, phone or
  email, an optional goal ("no medical details"), tick the privacy notice, Send my request.
  "Request sent" says it isn't booked until Frank confirms, and offers one tap to tell
  him on Instagram (the message is copied; WhatsApp with the text typed in once his number
  is in the app). No answers are lost without a connection: the form keeps them and says so.
- **For Frank:** Coach tools > Booking requests, behind a 6-digit PIN (Victor has it; Frank
  can change it there). Waiting / Confirmed / Declined lists; Confirm, Decline (Cancel for a
  confirmed one), Delete; a tap on the contact opens WhatsApp, a mail or a call with a
  message ready. **His weekly times** are set there too: one window a day, cut into
  back-to-back 2-hour sessions, offered from 12 hours to 4 weeks ahead. They start empty:
  nobody can book until he fills them in.
- **Rules:** times in The Hague's time zone. A pending or confirmed request holds its time
  (no double booking, enforced by the database); declined frees it. Spam limit: 5 requests
  an hour from one connection, 20 an hour in total.
- **Where the data is:** our Supabase project (servers in the United States), `frank_*`
  tables nobody can read directly; only checked functions. Requests are deleted 30 days
  after the session date. The SQL: `supabase/migrations/20261024120000_frank_booking.sql`.
- **Not in it yet:** a message to Frank when a request comes in (he checks his screen, or
  the visitor taps "Tell Frank"); a visitor seeing "confirmed" in the app (Frank messages
  them); payment for the session.
