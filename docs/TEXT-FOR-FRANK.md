# Text for Frank to approve

These lines are new or changed in the app. They wait for Frank's yes: until he
gives it, they are drafts. Each table is one screen: where the line shows, the
new text, and what it said before. The last table lists three older lines for
him to check.

"At risk" below means someone who may be under 18, is pregnant (or gave birth
in the last six months) or has a medical condition: a yes to the health
question about the heart, a chronic condition, medicine or medically
supervised activity, or the matching switch on the Today screen's food card.
